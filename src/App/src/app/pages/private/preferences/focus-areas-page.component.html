About Focus Areas

Focus areas are topics that are automatically identified based on your activities, such as marking presentations as favorites or checking in to sessions.

Weight Over Time: Focus areas gradually lose weight over a 3-year period. Recent activities have more influence on your interests than older ones.

Personalized Recommendations: These focus areas help us recommend presentations and sessions that match your interests.

Confirm Your Interests: You can confirm a focus area to indicate it's truly a topic of interest. Confirmed topics will always maintain maximum weight, regardless of time.
